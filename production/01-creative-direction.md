# 01 — Series Creative Direction

## Series A: BOARDROOM-READY — Finance Interview Simulations
**Brand:** CloudERP Accounting & Business Solutions · **Market:** Nigeria · **Language:** English (Nigerian)

---

### 1. Concept

A calm, credible, beautifully lit Lagos boardroom. Across a table, a senior finance leader interviews a candidate for a real role level. Every episode answers **one genuine interview question** well enough that a viewer could reuse the structure the same afternoon — and quietly proves that CloudERP's team lives inside this territory (close discipline, IFRS depth, controls, audit readiness).

The series earns attention through **usefulness first**, brand second. Nobody performs the brand. The brand owns the room.

> **On-screen framing (mandatory):** every episode is captioned *"AI-generated interview simulation for educational purposes."* It never implies a real hiring event. The candidate is a fictional character; quantified results he cites are labelled *illustrative*.

### 2. Audience & viewer promise

- **Primary:** middle and senior finance/accounting executives in Nigeria (Finance Managers, Financial Controllers, Heads of Finance, CFO-track professionals).
- **Secondary (commercial):** CEOs/MDs and group executives who *hire* or *replace* that layer — the people who buy CloudERP's advisory, fractional CFO and implementation work.
- **Viewer promise:** *"One real interview question, answered the way a panel wants to hear it — in about a minute."*

### 3. Tone

Professional, approachable, practical, confident. Speech is measured, warm Lagos corporate English — never rushed, never theatrical. No hype, no "you won't believe this", no fear-mongering. The interviewer is respectful and sharp; the candidate is composed, never smug. Small human details: a thinking pause, a nod, a half-smile at a well-landed point.

### 4. Creative principles

1. **The question is the hook.** Video opens on the interviewer asking it inside ~2 seconds. No logo sting, no "welcome back".
2. **One question, one answer, one takeaway.** Structure shown via a quiet on-screen topic chip and a closing takeaway card — not forced acronym theatre.
3. **Condense, never distort.** Approved answers are shortened for time but technical meaning, conditions and qualifications survive intact.
4. **The brand is the setting, not the script.** Logo on the coffee cup/wall plaque/end card; brand colours in wardrobe accents and graphics. Dialogue never advertises.
5. **Restraint reads as seniority.** Minimal camera movement, no whip pans, no kinetic captions. Captions are clean, lower-third, high contrast.
6. **Realism discipline.** Natural blinking/breathing, believable skin, correct eyelines, plausible hands. Prefer locked-off shots + slow push-ins over ambitious movement.

### 5. Signature set — "The CloudERP Room"

One repeatable room renders across all episodes (also usable for panel format later):
- Contemporary Lagos executive boardroom; floor-to-ceiling window band with soft daylight and a blurred Island skyline; warm neutral walls (warm grey), walnut table, two guest chairs window-side, **one host chair** camera-left.
- Brand presence: a small deep-navy wall plaque with the CloudERP placeholder lockup behind the host (readable but not dominant), a navy mug on the table, a teal folio/notebook in front of the interviewer.
- Wardrobe accents carry the palette (see brand system): interviewer wears teal accent; candidate wears a muted gold tie accent. This makes the brand felt without logos in every frame.
- **Reference plate:** `production/assets/set/SET-lagos-boardroom_ref.jpg` — reused for every episode's prompts for environmental consistency.

### 6. Recurring on-screen graphics language

| Element | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Cold-open hook text | Appears 0.0–2.0s over the establishing two-shot; ≤7 words; white on 80% navy scrim chip. |
| Topic chip | Small navy pill, top-safe zone: e.g., `GROUP FINANCIAL CONTROLLER · Q6 — FIRST 90 DAYS`. |
| Lower thirds | Name + fictional role title of current speaker; enters on first line, holds 4s, exits. |
| Captions | Burned-in, 1–2 lines, sentence-case, max ~42 chars/line, Inter SemiBold, white w/ navy 65% backing band; positioned above YouTube UI safe area. |
| Takeaway card | 3.0–3.5s full-frame navy card: one-sentence lesson in Sora SemiBold + tiny logo lockup. |
| End card | 3.5–4s: logo lockup, one CTA line, website route in words, disclosure microcopy. |
| "Illustrative figures" tag | Small gold-outline chip, bottom-left above captions, shown while the candidate cites quantified results. |

### 7. Music & sound

- Optional low-volume underscore: sparse, warm corporate bed (−24 to −28 LUFS under dialogue), licensed or library-cleared only; **off by default** in Wave 1 (dialogue clarity first).
- Room tone: minimal; a whisper of HVAC for realism, −40 dB under speech.
- No whooshes, no dings. Transitions are clean cuts.

### 8. Format variations mapped for this series (per programme §14)

- *"How would you answer this interview question?"* → default frame for every episode.
- *"A common mistake — and a stronger approach"* → Wave 2 candidates (e.g., Q7 controls).
- *"Why this accounting judgment matters"* → Wave 2/3 (Q4 IFRS 15, Q17 IFRS vs US GAAP).
- Hook A/B plans: see episode files GFC-E03 and GFC-E06 (two openers each, same lesson).
