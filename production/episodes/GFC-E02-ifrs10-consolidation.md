## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "Walk me through how you approach group consolidation under IFRS ten."

**[0.5 s thinking pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "I work through five layers. First, the structure assessment: for every entity I confirm control under IFRS ten — power over the investee, exposure to variable returns, and the ability to use that power to affect those returns. Second, uniformity: common accounting policies, aligned reporting dates, and a group close calendar so every subsidiary's numbers are final by day three. Third, eliminations: intercompany trade, loans, dividends and unrealised profit — run through a standing eliminations matrix, so nothing rests on memory. Fourth, measurement: goodwill and non-controlling interests under IFRS three, with impairment testing under IAS thirty-six. Fifth, translation under IAS twenty-one: assets and liabilities at closing rate, income and expenses at average rates, differences to the cumulative translation reserve in equity. The output is a consolidation pack where every eliminated balance is reconciled — that's how you hold a five-day group close with zero unexplained differences."

**[Takeaway card]** "Consolidation is a system: control, uniformity, eliminations, measurement, translation."

*[*] Contingency trims if final audio exceeds 76 s: remove "— so nothing rests on memory" (keep on takeaway card); remove "group" in "five-day group close".
