## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "How would you prepare this company for an IPO?"

**[0.6 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "An IPO-ready finance function stands on three pillars. First, the financial statements: two to three years of clean, consistent IFRS financials — or US GAAP, depending on the venue [*1] — with the complex areas nailed down early: revenue, share-based payments, leases, expected credit losses and capitalised development costs. Every legal entity consolidated, intercompany mapped and reconciled — one untidy subsidiary can slip an IPO timeline, and it is entirely avoidable. Second, controls and governance: ICFR documentation in the SOX mould [*2], a delegation-of-authority framework, and an audit committee that has been rehearsed. Appoint the auditors early: a first audit to that standard takes longer than most companies expect. Third, operational readiness: a quarterly external-reporting cadence, close discipline — a five-day close is exactly the muscle an IPO-track company needs — and investor-grade management reporting. My edge is fourteen years in audit: I build to audit standard from day one — not retrofit it in year three."

**[*1][*2] Trims if >76 s:** *1 venue aside is preparer-context not core; *2 inner detail retained in captions' takeaway instead.

**[Takeaway card]** "IPO readiness = clean financials · rehearsed controls · a reporting cadence that runs before the bell. Start the audit early."
