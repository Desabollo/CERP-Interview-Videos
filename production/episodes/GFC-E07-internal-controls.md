## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip: "Controls fail in the gaps."]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "Tell me about a time you designed or strengthened internal controls."

**[0.5 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "At my current group, rapid multi-entity growth had left the control environment uneven — inconsistent approvals, and reconciliation discipline that varied by entity. As group head, I needed a framework strong enough for the board, without strangling the business. So I designed and rolled out a group controls framework: a segregation-of-duties matrix mapped to actual roles, not job descriptions; delegation-of-authority limits by transaction type and value; standardised approval workflows; mandatory monthly reconciliations with documented review sign-off; and a close checklist that operates as a control document. Then I trained the team, and built compliance into performance expectations. The result: financial risk came down materially, audits ran cleaner, and the board got a control environment it could rely on. The lesson from fourteen years in audit: controls fail in the gaps between people and process — so keep them simple, and monitor with data, not memos."

**[Takeaway card]** "SoD by real roles · DoA by value · reconciliations with sign-off · checklist as control. Then train — and monitor with data, not memos."
