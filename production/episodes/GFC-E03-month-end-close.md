## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip (A or B)]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "How do you run the month-end close — and how have you improved close speed?"

**[0.5 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "My philosophy: the close is a process you run all month — not an event you survive. The mechanics: a close calendar with named owners and deadlines down to hours. Work moves before period end — recurring journals automated, accruals standardised, high-volume reconciliations maintained continuously. Sub-ledgers lock at cut-off, and executives get a flash result within forty-eight hours. The review layer is flux analysis — every material movement versus budget and prior period explained before the numbers are published. After each close, a short retrospective fixes the worst friction point before the next cycle. That discipline took our group close down twenty to thirty per cent, to five working days — with better quality, not less: fewer late adjustments, a cleaner audit trail. The next lever is rule-based auto-posting and exception-based reconciliation — so the team's time goes to judgment, not routine processing."

**[Takeaway card]** "Close fast by closing all month: pre-close work, locked cut-off, 48-hour flash, flux review — then fix one friction point per cycle."
