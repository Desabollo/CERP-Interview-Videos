## B. Script (exact dialogue)

> Performance directions are **not** spoken. [brackets] = direction only. TTS-stable spellings used in voice prompts (Section D).

**[COLD OPEN — two-shot, hook chip on screen]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "Mister Adeyemi — tell me about yourself, and why you're the right fit for this Group Financial Controller role."

**[CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide) — brief 0.6 s thinking pause, then:]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "I'm a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria, with over twenty years of progressive finance leadership. For the past four years, I've effectively run group controllership for a diversified, multi-entity portfolio — group consolidation under IFRS ten, statutory reporting, internal controls and audit management — and I rebuilt the close process so the group reports within five working days, a twenty to thirty per cent improvement in cycle time. Before that, fourteen years leading statutory audits, forensic investigations and due diligence in public practice — so I build reporting to audit standard from day one, not as an afterthought. I've also run distributed, multi-currency finance operations — donor-funded budgets above five million dollars, and fully remote work for US entities. What I bring is the full controllership stack: technical IFRS depth, an auditor's eye for governance, a systems mindset, and finance teams that deliver reliably."

**[Cut to takeaway card — no spoken line]**
**On-screen takeaway:** "Credential → scope → proof → fit. Answer in that order."
