## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip: "One group. Five currencies."]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "How do you handle multi-currency and multi-jurisdiction accounting?"

**[0.5 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "Technically, it starts with IAS twenty-one: each entity's functional currency follows its primary economic environment; foreign operations translate assets and liabilities at closing rate, and income and expenses at average rates, with the differences going to the cumulative translation reserve in equity; monetary items remeasure through profit or loss. And I track the IAS twenty-one amendments on lack of exchangeability — they matter in markets with currency distortions. Operationally, it's a treasury discipline: match the currency of receipts and payments, natural hedging, cash visibility across jurisdictions, and disciplined intercompany settlement. I've run dollar-denominated project finance against a naira cost base, and two US entities entirely remotely. For a distributed global business I'd add: a group treasury policy; transfer-pricing documentation aligned to intercompany flows; and consistent banking and ERP structures — so consolidation isn't a monthly archaeology project."

**[Takeaway card]** "Functional currency · closing vs average rates · CTA reserve in equity · then treasury discipline and TP documentation."
