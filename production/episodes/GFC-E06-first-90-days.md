## B. Script (LOCKED — previz audio generated from this text)

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "What would your first ninety days as Group Financial Controller look like?"

**[0.6 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "I run it in three phases — assess, design, institutionalise. Days one to thirty: map the entities, people, chart of accounts and systems; time the current close end-to-end; inventory reconciliations and controls. Then I publish a findings memo with quick wins — high-risk reconciliations, cut-off fixes, ERP functionality we already own. Days thirty-one to sixty: a redesigned close calendar with hour-level ownership; a consolidation pack and eliminations matrix; a controls matrix for segregation of duties and delegation limits; and policy papers for the highest-risk areas — revenue, leases, credit losses. Days sixty-one to ninety: run one full close under the new framework and measure it — close days, reconciliation completion, late adjustments. Publish the KPIs and deliver the first board-standard reporting pack. Success looks like: a faster close, key reconciliations one hundred per cent current, and a leadership team that trusts the numbers without a caveat."

**[Takeaway card]** "Ninety days, three phases: assess the engine, redesign it, prove it with one measured close."

*[*] Contingency trims if >76 s: drop "cut-off fixes," and "revenue, leases, credit losses" (retained on takeaway card conceptually).
