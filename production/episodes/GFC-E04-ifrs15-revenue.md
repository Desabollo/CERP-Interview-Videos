## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip: "Revenue is a judgment call."]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "How have you applied IFRS fifteen — and how would you apply it to our revenue model?"

**[0.5 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "At my current group, IFRS fifteen is everyday work: identifying performance obligations; assessing whether control transfers over time — typically where the asset has no alternative use and we hold an enforceable right to payment for performance to date; accounting for significant financing components; and constraining variable consideration. In a technology business, the judgment points are: subscription revenue as a stand-ready obligation, recognised ratably over the term; whether implementation or onboarding fees are distinct obligations or simply costs to fulfil; principal-versus-agent analysis, which drives gross-versus-net presentation; usage-based fees as constrained variable consideration; and disciplined cut-off over deferred revenue. My approach: codify it in a revenue policy paper with worked examples, map the billing systems to those obligations, and keep the audit trail complete. Having audited this standard for years, I know exactly where it gets challenged."

**[*] Trim if >76 s:** drop "map the billing systems to those obligations," (covered conceptually by takeaway).

**[Takeaway card]** "IFRS 15 in five judgments: obligations · ratable recognition · principal vs agent · constraint · cut-off. Then codify it."
