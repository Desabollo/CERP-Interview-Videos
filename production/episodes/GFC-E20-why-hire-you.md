## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "Finally, Mister Adeyemi — why should we hire you as our Group Financial Controller?"

**[0.6 s pause; slight smile — the question he came for]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "Three reasons. First, I already do this job at scale — group consolidation, IFRS statutory reporting, internal controls and audit management across a multi-entity portfolio, with a five-working-day close and a twenty to thirty per cent cycle improvement to show for it. Second, I bring an auditor's rigour — fourteen years leading statutory audits, forensic investigations and due diligence. I build reporting that survives scrutiny the first time — which matters enormously if you're heading toward public markets. Third, I'm a builder: of teams, controls, close processes and systems — across sectors and geographies, including fully remote, multi-currency work. A distributed, growing business is my natural habitat. I want to build the finance function a company at your stage deserves — and I have both the scars and the playbook from doing it."

**[Takeaway card]** "Close on three: proof at scale, a rigour others don't bring, and a builder's record — tied to their stage."
