## B. Script

**[COLD OPEN — hook chip: "Migrations fail before go-live."]**

**INT-01 (Adaeze):** "Describe an ERP implementation or system conversion you've led."

**[0.5 s pause]**

**CAND-GFC-01 (Olumide):** "I've led conversions at every career stage. In audit practice, I supervised multiple client conversions — chart-of-accounts mapping, master-data cleansing, opening-balance migration and reconciliation, parallel runs of critical cycles, training, and post-go-live support. In a donor-funded organisation, I re-engineered the budget-coding structure in the ERP to sharpen cost tracking and donor reporting. The rules I now apply by default: rationalise the chart of accounts before migrating anything; freeze and validate master data; reconcile opening balances account by account; parallel-run at least one full close before cutover; and over-invest in training — because adoption, not technology, determines success. For a distributed company I'd add two things: a clear data-ownership model, and IT general controls designed in from the start — because auditors test the system, not just its outputs."

**[Takeaway card]** "COA first · master data frozen · opening balances reconciled · one parallel close · train hard. Adoption decides."
