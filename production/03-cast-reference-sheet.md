## Shared performance direction (all characters)

- Natural blinking and breathing; small head movements; posture shifts at conversational beats.
- Only the active speaker articulates; listener reactions = subtle nod, blink, slight smile. **Never** synchronized nodding or fixed smiles.
- Eye contact: speaker → scene partner; on singles, eyeline just off-camera toward partner's seat (camera-left for candidate, camera-right for interviewer).
- 0.4–0.8 s thinking pause before substantive answers; no "um/ah" fillers scripted.
- Hand gestures: purposeful, 1–2 per sentence maximum, inside frame margins.

## Pronunciation key (used in all voice prompts)

`IFRS` = "eye-eff-ar-ess" · `IAS 21` = "eye-ay-ess twenty-one" · `IFRS 10` = "IFRS ten" · `ECL` = "ee-cee-ell" ·
`₦18bn` = "eighteen billion naira" · `5-day close` = "five-working-day close" · `CEO/CFO` = letters spoken ·
`ICAN` = "eye-can" · numbers ≥1000 spelled out in words for TTS stability.
